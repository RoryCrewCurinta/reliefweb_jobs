Measure Count

Core means the job is the topic: it is the main subject of the role, usually named in the title or purpose. Core or duty adds jobs where the topic is a substantial part of the work but not its main subject: broader, and less reliable. Core is the safer default. Four topics are counted as core only: Partnerships & partner capacity, Needs assessment & analysis, Safety & security management, and Compliance, audit & risk.

      How to read this

      What is counted

      • Advertised posts on ReliefWeb, including expired ones. Not hires, and not all humanitarian hiring.
      • Every ad was read by a language model (OpenAI GPT-6 Luna) and tagged against a list of 65 defined topics, in English, French and Spanish. A topic counts when the job itself involves it, not when the word appears in the organisation's description of itself or in standard recruitment wording.
      • Each tag has a level. Core: the job is the topic. Duty: a substantial part of the job. Skills asked for and passing mentions are not counted.
      • A specialist topic also counts under its parent: sexual and reproductive health, epidemics and MHPSS under Health; MPCA, cash coordination and group cash transfers under CVA. Do not add a parent to its specialists.

      Cautions

      • Use shares for trends. Total posting fell sharply in 2024–25, around the US funding cuts, so counts fall for almost every topic.
      • Unusually quiet months are flagged in the data. The cause cannot be told from this data.
      • Some organisations moved recruitment to their own sites. A collapse in one organisation's volume may mean that rather than a hiring freeze.

      Topics to read carefully

      • The tags are a model's reading, not a hand count. In checks, 98% of its core tags were confirmed by a stronger model, and it found 87% of the core topics that model found. Counts therefore run a little low; trends are more reliable than levels.
      • Core or duty is noisier: about three in four duty tags were confirmed.
      • Research, studies & evaluation covers research and study roles and evaluation consultancies in general, not only rigorous impact evaluations.
      • Ads with almost no text (about 0.3%) were not read and carry no topics.
      • Watchlist topics are small. They are shown as counts and should be read as signals.
      • Rankings need at least 20 matching posts in each period compared.
