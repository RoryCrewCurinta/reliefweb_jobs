Terms used on the dashboard

Advert, or postOne job advert published on ReliefWeb. Not a hire.
Share of advertsAdverts with a topic, divided by all adverts in the same period. Used for trends so a shrinking market does not look like a shrinking topic.
CoreThe topic or theme is central to the role. This is the default measure used on this site.
DutyThe topic is a substantial part of the work but not its main subject.
Core or dutyCounts adverts where the topic is either core or a duty. Broader and noisier than core alone.
Exploratory themesEight small topics with too few adverts for a reliable trend. Shown as counts only, and left out of rankings.
Parent topicA topic that includes its specialists. Health includes SRH, epidemics and MHPSS. CVA includes MPCA, cash coordination and group cash transfers.
Job typeReliefWeb's own field: job, consultancy, internship or volunteer opportunity.
Career categoryReliefWeb's own field for the function of the role, such as programme management or logistics.
ExperienceReliefWeb's own field: 0 to 2, 3 to 4, 5 to 9, or 10 or more years.
High income, low or middle incomeBased on the World Bank's income group for the organisation's home country, as ReliefWeb records it. The World Bank groups countries as high, upper middle, lower middle or low income by gross national income per person. Low or middle income means any of the last three. The October 2026 classification is used for every year.
International NGOAn NGO whose home country, as ReliefWeb records it, is high income.
Locally based NGOAn NGO whose home country is low or middle income and which places at least 80% of its located adverts there. A proxy for national NGOs.
Regional NGOAn NGO based in a low or middle income country that advertises across several countries.
International organisationReliefWeb's type for UN agencies and other intergovernmental bodies.
Topic concentrationHow much more often an organisation advertises for a topic than the sector does: the topic's share of the organisation's adverts, divided by its share of all adverts. 2× means twice as often.
